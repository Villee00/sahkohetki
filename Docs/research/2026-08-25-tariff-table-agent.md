# Finnish household electricity distribution tariffs, 2026

Checkpoint research snapshot, 2026-08-25.

## Scope and interpretation

The comparison source is Suomen Omakotiliitto's 2026 **Yleissiirto** table, compiled for the association by KTI Kiinteistötieto Oy: [source PDF/XLSX attachment served by STT Info][OL]. The attachment is served as a PDF even though its filename describes an XLSX. It lists 77 Finnish distribution-network companies.

The source table is a 2026 snapshot: the attached file was created on 2026-05-04 and the accompanying release was published on 2026-05-06. Its footnotes say that the table follows the general-transfer price for **3 x 25 A** and that the energy charge is reported **without electricity tax**. The values below therefore mean:

- fixed fee: euros/month, VAT-inclusive;
- energy charge: cents/kWh, VAT-inclusive network/distribution charge, **excluding electricity tax**;
- decimal points are used here for normalization; the source uses decimal commas.

Do not add electricity tax to the energy-charge column. Electricity tax is a separate statutory line on the network bill. Where an operator's official page only publishes a tax-inclusive total, that is marked as a definition mismatch rather than silently converted.

The comparison is operator-specific, not city-specific. A municipality can contain more than one distribution operator, and an operator can serve multiple municipalities. The applicable tariff is determined by the electricity distribution network at the usage point.

## Source and extraction notes

- [OL] is the Omakotiliitto/KTI comparison attachment: [Sähkön siirtohinnat 2026 (kaikki yhtiöt)_lisätty mökkilaskenta.xlsx/PDF](https://www.sttinfo.fi/files/67340313/72011769/375325/fi).
- The accompanying release is [Sähkön siirtomaksujen rakenne ja hinta vaativat avoimuutta kuluttajille, sanoo Omakotiliitto](https://www.sttinfo.fi/tiedote/72011769/sahkon-siirtomaksujen-rakenne-ja-hinta-vaativat-avoimuutta-kuluttajille-sanoo-omakotiliitto?lang=fi&publisherId=67340313), published 2026-05-06. It says the comparison covers all 77 Finnish distribution-network companies and that distribution prices depend on the customer's place of residence.
- The source table's own footnote is the controlling extraction rule: `yleissiirto`, fuse size `3 x 25 A`, and energy charge without electricity tax. Official tariffs often use equivalent labels such as `Yleissähkö`, `Yleistariffi`, `Yksiaikasähkö`, or `Normaltariff`.
- Annual fixed fees in official PDFs were divided by 12 and rounded to the two decimals used by the comparison table.
- A source row can be historically correct for the Omakotiliitto snapshot while not being the operator's tariff on 2026-08-25. The table deliberately retains the comparison snapshot and records later tariff changes in the note instead of replacing the requested 2026 comparison values.
- Product/category caveats are material: Lahti's row is a power-based product, Kajave and Vimpeli distinguish usage-point categories, and Muonio/Tornio official pages expose totals that include electricity tax rather than the source's separate network-energy charge.
